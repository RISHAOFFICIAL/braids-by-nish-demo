Meet the hands behind the braids.

Nish started braiding because she loved the way a fresh set of braids could change how a woman walks into a room. That feeling never got old, so she turned it into Braids by Nish.

Now she is one of Detroit's most followed braiders, with 71,000 followers on Instagram watching every style she drops. Her stitch braids built the following: parts so clean they look drawn on, stitches so tight the braids lay flat for weeks.

Every style is done with care for your edges, your scalp, and your time. No rushing, no cutting corners. Just beautiful work, every single time.
